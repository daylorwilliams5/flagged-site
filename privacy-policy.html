FLAGGED

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

Short version: FLAGGED collects nothing. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not make network requests of any kind. The only data that exists is your own gameplay progress, and it never leaves your iPhone.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the iOS app FLAGGED (bundle identifier com.daylorwilliams.flagged), published by Daylor Williams, an individual developer. In this policy, "the app," "FLAGGED," "we," and "I" all refer to that app and its developer.

Information we collect

None. FLAGGED does not collect, request, receive, or transmit any personal information or usage information.

Specifically, the app does not:

  • ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or phone number;
  • collect your device identifiers, advertising identifier (IDFA), or IP address;
  • request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, health data, or any other sensitive device permission;
  • include any analytics, telemetry, attribution, crash-reporting, advertising, or marketing SDK;
  • display advertisements or offer in-app purchases or subscriptions;
  • contain any user accounts, chat, comments, messaging, or other user-generated content.

We do not transmit anything

FLAGGED is a fully offline app. It contains no networking code and makes no requests to any server — not ours, and not anyone else's. There is no backend service, no cloud sync, no leaderboard service, and no remote configuration. The app works identically in Airplane Mode.

The country flags in the daily game are standard Unicode flag characters rendered by the fonts already on your iPhone. A small number of historical flags, used in an optional extra mode, are vector drawings bundled inside the app itself. Either way, the app does not download images or contact an image host.

What is stored on your device

So the game can remember your progress between launches, FLAGGED saves a small amount of gameplay data in the app's own private storage area on your iPhone. This data stays inside the app's sandbox on your device. It is not sent anywhere, it is not readable by other apps, and neither the developer nor any third party can access it.

What is storedWhy
Completed game results — the date played, the mode (Easy, Hard, or the optional extra mode), your score and number of correct answers, your completion time, when you finished, and the answers you submitted for each of the ten flags To show your result screen and answer review, and to enforce one game per day
Your daily streak, derived from which days you completed To show your current streak
Hint usage for the current day (a count, up to two) To limit hints to two per day
An in-progress game, if you leave mid-game (which question you are on and the answers you have given so far) So you can resume today's game where you left off; it is discarded once the day changes
Your preferred mode (Easy or Hard) To remember which mode the Play button starts
Whether the optional extra mode has been unlocked on this device (a yes or no) So you do not have to re-enter the access code every time

That is the complete list. Nothing else is written to storage, and none of it identifies you.

Sharing your result

The result screen has an optional "Share result" button. If — and only if — you tap it, iOS opens its standard share sheet and you choose where the text goes (Messages, Notes, a social app, the clipboard, and so on). The shared text is spoiler-free: it contains the daily challenge number, the mode you played, a grid of green and red squares, your score out of ten, and your streak. It does not contain any personal information.

The app itself does not send this text anywhere. Once you pick a destination, the text is handled by the app you chose and by iOS, under their own privacy policies. If you never tap Share, nothing is ever handed off.

Settings contains one other optional hand-off: a button that requests access to an extra mode. Tapping it opens your email app with a short message already written and addressed to our support address. The app does not send it and cannot send it — it only opens your email app, exactly as if you had typed the address in yourself. Anything you then choose to send is ordinary email correspondence between you and us, handled by your email provider under its own privacy policy, and it is not data the app collects. We use it only to reply to you. If you never tap the button, nothing is handed off.

Third parties

There are none. FLAGGED integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting services. No data about you is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because no such data exists.

The app is built with open-source frameworks (Expo and React Native) that ship as code inside the app. They are not services, they do not receive data from the app, and the app does not enable any of their optional network features.

Tracking

FLAGGED does not track you. It does not use the advertising identifier, does not link any data to your identity, and does not share data with data brokers. The app's privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking: false with no tracking domains and no collected data types. Because the app performs no tracking, it does not present App Tracking Transparency prompts.

Children's privacy

FLAGGED is a general-audience geography quiz that is safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, so there is nothing to disclose, share, or delete under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR, or similar laws. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no external links, no messaging, and no user-generated content, so there is no way for a child (or anyone) to disclose personal information through it.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is no server-side data to retain and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

The local gameplay data described above remains on your iPhone for as long as the app is installed. You can remove all of it at any time:

  • Delete the app. Deleting FLAGGED from your iPhone permanently removes every piece of data it stored, including all results, streaks, hint usage, any in-progress game, and your mode preference. Nothing is left behind and nothing can be recovered.

Reinstalling the app starts you over with an empty history. There is no backup or account to restore from, so this deletion is final.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, port, and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you. The only data connected to your use of FLAGGED lives on your own device and is entirely under your control, as described above. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

Security

Since no data leaves your device, there is no transmission to intercept and no server to breach. Data stored by the app is protected by iOS itself: it lives in the app's private sandbox and is covered by your device's own encryption and passcode protection. The app additionally disables arbitrary and local cleartext network loads at the operating-system level.

Changes to this policy

If FLAGGED ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if a future version adds an optional account or online leaderboard — this policy will be updated before that version is released, the "Last updated" date at the top will change, and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated to match. Material changes will be described here. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
